Help / Glossary

Z-score

How far a value is from its recent norm, in standard deviations.

Rolling window

The previous 60 valid observations used as the comparison baseline. Volume is log1p-transformed first.

Volume vs price anomaly

A volume flag concerns trading activity; a price flag concerns daily return. A flag is raised when |z| is above 3.0.

Local news match

At least one local news record for the same ticker within ±7 days. The dashboard distinguishes news available by the flag date from later context. Neither proximity nor absence proves a cause.

Watchlist tags

MARKET_WIDE means several tickers were flagged together; COMPANY_SPECIFIC means a more isolated signal in this triage output.

AI draft assessment

An external model’s draft opinion. Human review is required.

Limitations

The labelled set is small, local news coverage can be incomplete, and flags are not findings.
