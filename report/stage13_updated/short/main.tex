\section*{Abstract}
The final project screens stored BVMT market observations, links available
news context, and presents a human review workflow in a React Terminal.
The frozen prior-only detector, \code{reference_v1}, yields 1,414 flags:
1,399 on company shares and 15 on PX1. It hits one of five eligible
expanded events. Phase 2 is the predeclared expanded-label comparison. Its held-out set
contains one positive; no candidate qualified for adoption. The selected
\code{robust_z3} is a median/MAD robust z-score with cutoff 3. These are case studies, not accuracy estimates.
