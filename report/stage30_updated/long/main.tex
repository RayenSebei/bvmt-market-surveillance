\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This report presents the final BVMT statistical market-surveillance project.
Stored market and ilboursa news records pass through a frozen prior-only
reference detector, news-context matching, a refined review queue, and a
React, TypeScript, and Tailwind Terminal served by the read-only Flask API.

The market file contains 67,035 observations in 85 series: 83 company shares
and two indices. The frozen reference produces 1,414 union flags, of which
548 have local news context. The refined queue has 16 rows. Its original
three-event result remains one hit; the expanded sourced evaluation has
one hit among five eligible positives, with only one held-out positive.

The Phase 2 protocol preceded candidate code. Six registered variants,
baselines, Wilson intervals, random-placement tests, source audits, and
data-quality checks were reported without retuning the reference.
No candidate met the adoption rule, so \code{reference_v1} is retained.
These are screening case studies, not an accuracy estimate or a finding
of misconduct.
