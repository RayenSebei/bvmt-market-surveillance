# Phase 2 protocol amendments

## 2026-11-17: source-alignment rule

This amendment is recorded before applying the label corrections identified in `docs/LABEL_AUDIT.md` or inspecting their effect on Phase 2 results. It does not alter the frozen candidate rules, thresholds, windows, splits, or adoption criteria in `docs/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_v2.md`.

> Label corrections that fix a mismatch between an event row and its cited source are applied as the audit recommends, regardless of the effect on any detector's results. The rule is applied identically to every method. Corrections are never chosen by their effect on hits.

Specifically, the audit calls for using the cited UADH suspension date, distinguishing the Syphax public-report date from its court-ruling date, removing an unsupported takeover explanation and negative-control label from ADWYA, and reporting the shared PGH/LNDOR notice both as two ticker windows and as one correlated control cluster. These are source and accounting corrections, not detector changes.
